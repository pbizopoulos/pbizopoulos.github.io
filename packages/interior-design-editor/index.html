Sun & shadows

Orientation is the bearing of the layout’s north wall: 0° true north, 90° east. Negative coordinates mean south or west. Set the UTC offset for your location and date, including daylight saving.

Automatic fixture lighting

Default location: Athens. Editing approximates diffuse daylight from exterior openings. Walls, ceilings, and floors block direct light even when hidden for editing. Editing adds room-aware daylight fill to direct light from all fixtures. Exposure brightens the image without adding light. Use first person to view interiors. All fixtures switch on when the sun is below the horizon and off during daylight, regardless of camera position. Fast quality reduces rendering resolution. Large scenes take longer.
