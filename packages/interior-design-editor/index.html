DESIGN GUIDE

Quick guide

  • ROOM living 8x6 AT 0,0 — size and position in grid cells.
  • FLOOR 1 — subsequent rooms sit 3m above floor 0; levels 0–7.
  • LAYOUT living … END — rows north→south, cells west→east. Use | between cells and . for blanks.
  • stairs — place beneath a room on the next floor; openings are automatic. elevator — place aligned stops of the same size and rotation on two or more consecutive floors, including the top floor. First person: E up, Shift+E down nearby.
  • sofa@90 rotates; desk[1.5x0.7x0.8] sizes in metres; desk(work_on_top) adds a named top layout. Fronts face south at 0°, east at 90°, north at 180°, and west at 270°; top layouts rotate with their parent.
  • Add a product URL after an object: sofa<https://www.ikea.com/product-page> or desk(work_on_top)~north<https://jysk.com/product-page>. Paste the specific item's full HTTP(S) address between angle brackets. Each object can have its own link, including objects on top and MOUNT fixtures. Click an object in the preview or its item in the layout editor to keep its details open, then choose “View product”. Close the details with × or Escape. Product links are included when copying layouts or sharing a scene.
  • sink~north places its back against the north wall, facing into the room. Use ~north, ~east, ~south, or ~west after the whole object, e.g. kitchen_counter(coffee_on_top)~north. The cell sets the position along the wall; depth sets the distance from it. Wall placement also supports shared walls. Use it on room-level furniture without @ rotation; objects on top inherit the parent's position and rotation. Stairs, elevators, and plain objects stay at cell centers.
  • GRID 0.85 sets cell size. WALLS north west, DOORS west, STYLE blue style the latest room. WINDOWS north west chooses window walls; WINDOWS none removes windows. Use walls longer than 2.2m without doors. Omit WINDOWS for automatic windows.
  • BALCONY terrace 8x3 AT 0,6 + RAILS south east adds outdoor space.
  • Drag to orbit; scroll to zoom. Walls and ceilings have separate collapse/restore controls. Ceilings start collapsed for an open overview; balconies stay uncovered. Select a floor to inspect it. First person: drag to look, W/S to move forward/back, A/D to turn left/right (or use arrow keys), Esc to exit. Walls, furniture, and floor edges block movement; E / Shift+E uses nearby connectors.
  • Hover over an object in the layout editor or preview to highlight it in the other view. Edits update the preview automatically. Choose an example to explore; copy a share URL to reopen your current design; ? opens help.
  • Click a gutter arrow to fold or expand a layout, room settings, or floor section. Collapse / Expand controls the whole layout. Collapsed sections remain visible in the preview and copy in full; hovering a scene object reveals it in the layout editor. Undo/redo and Ctrl/Cmd+Enter work in the editor.
