GRID 0.5 sets metres per cell.
ROOM living 8x6 AT 0,0 defines a room.

Set WALLS, WINDOWS, and DOORS with north, east, south, west, or none. Openings sit at the wall centre.

LAYOUT living begins the furniture grid. Separate cells with |, use . for empty space, finish with END.

sofa[1.8x0.9x0.8]@90 sets width × depth × height and rotation. bed~north places furniture against a wall. Add <https://…> for a product link.

SURFACE wood sets flooring. HEIGHT 2.8 sets ceiling height. FLOOR 1 begins the upper storey at 3 m. LIGHT ceiling_light AT 3,3 POWER 18 adds a fixture.

Use BALCONY or GARDEN for outdoor areas. SITE grass 5, FACADE plaster, and ROOF flat set the exterior. coffee_table(objects_on_top) uses a child layout.
